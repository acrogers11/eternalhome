A short playlist for August. Summer is coming to an end and life is filled with a more serious air. I reject change because I feel always almost just settled into the present. Maybe that’s the truth that I have never accepted, that there will always be an underlying uneasiness, but perhaps that is what makes life exciting. I will miss you so much, summer. Thank you for your lessons and for all the days of adventure.

1. Big Business Monkey - Daniel Johnston
2. When your Lover has Gone - Russ Garcia 
3. Sorrow is my Name - Connie Converse 
4. This Guitar (Can't Keep From Crying) (Platinum Weird Version) - George Harrison 
5. Te Recuerdo, Amanda - Victor Jara
6. Awaiting on You all Day (Demo 1; take 1) - George Harrison
