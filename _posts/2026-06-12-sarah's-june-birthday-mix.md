June is my favorite month of the whole year. It’s the beginning of summer, ripe with excitement, and feels like the first days of birth. It evokes a feeling of walking barefoot on thick grass or eating sticky fruit, then taking a dip to wash it off. I wanted to make a playlist to accompany that. It’s always kinda hard for me to make these because I tend to think too rigidly about themes, but this time around I had a lot of fun just playing around. Anyway, enjoy!
