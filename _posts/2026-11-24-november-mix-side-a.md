i've been sulking hardcore this week because one of the cranks on my bike is badly cracked and i've been waiting on a replacement. i think it may finally come in tomorrow, but i think this past week is the longest i've gone without biking in months, and ironically i signed up to do my first brevet in december. i hope i can make it happen short a week of training, but we'll have to wait and see. trying to stay positive despite myriad instances of bad luck, but so it goes. 

anyway, this week i heard a mix on NTS of eastern european music that's since sent me down a deep rabbit hole, a couple tracks from my investigation can be heard below. winter is coming along just fine all things considered. the crops i've been storing from the harvest are coming in handy now that the weather is turning for the worst in the city. let's hope i can put in some miles this next week. there may even be a different bike on the way sometime soon... peace and love to everyone.
